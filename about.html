FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Codry — from appointments to insurance.

01

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we accept all major insurance providers. We also offer direct billing for your convenience. Please bring your insurance card to your first visit.

02

How do I book an appointment?

You can book online through our website, call us directly, or walk in. Same-day appointments are available for urgent cases.

03

What are your operating hours?

We are open Monday–Saturday, 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM. We are closed on Fridays and public holidays.

04

Do you have female doctors?

Yes, we have female specialists in Gynecology, Pediatrics, and Dermatology. You can request a female doctor when booking.

05

How long is the waiting time?

Average wait time is under 15 minutes. We prioritize urgent cases and offer flexible scheduling to minimize delays.

06

Do you have a pharmacy?

Yes, we have a fully stocked on-site pharmacy open during clinic hours. You can get your prescriptions filled before you leave.
